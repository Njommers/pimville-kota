# FOODIE ZONE PIMVILLE – ACCURATE RESTAURANT WEBSITE

Build a premium, professional restaurant website for the real Foodie Zone business in Pimville, Soweto, South Africa.

This website is being developed as a sales demonstration by NextWave Web Solutions.

## 1. BUSINESS IDENTITY

Restaurant: Foodie Zone  
Location: 10681 Bhele Street, Pimville, Soweto, Gauteng, South Africa.

Publicly listed telephone number: +27 69 836 3353 (requires owner confirmation).

Instagram: @the_zone_for_the_foodie (requires owner confirmation).

Do not invent business details.

## 2. WEBSITE DESIGN

Create a modern South African fast-food restaurant experience.

Design requirements:
- Premium black, orange, white and subtle gold colour scheme.
- Attractive food photography.
- Modern typography.
- Professional navigation.
- Clean product cards.
- Responsive mobile-first design.
- Smooth animations.
- Fast loading.

The website should look like a professionally developed restaurant website, not a generic AI template.

## 3. HOMEPAGE

Headline: GOOD FOOD. GREAT MOMENTS.

Supporting text:
Discover delicious meals and satisfy your cravings at Foodie Zone, Pimville.

Include:
- Explore Menu button.
- Featured meals.
- Food photography.
- About Foodie Zone section.
- Contact and location section.
- WhatsApp ordering button, initially using a placeholder until the owner confirms the ordering number.

## 4. MENU

Build the menu around publicly mentioned Foodie Zone offerings.

Use these known menu references:
- Beef Kota.
- Rib Kota.
- Beef Dagwood.
- Cheesy Smash Burger.
- Steaks.

Create categories for:
- Kotas.
- Dagwoods.
- Burgers.
- Steaks.
- Chips and sides.
- Drinks.

IMPORTANT:
The exact product variations, ingredients, portion sizes and current prices must be confirmed by the restaurant owner.

Do not invent additional products or represent sample prices as actual restaurant prices.

Use clearly labelled sample products only where necessary to demonstrate the design. Make all sample prices editable.

## 5. PRODUCT DISPLAY

Every product card should have:
- Food image.
- Product name.
- Short description, only where verified.
- Price in South African Rand.
- Add to Cart button.

Use appetising, realistic food images that match the actual product.

Do not use unrelated food images.

## 6. SHOPPING CART

Implement a working cart with:
- Add to Cart.
- Remove products.
- Increase and decrease quantities.
- Automatic subtotal.
- Order summary.
- Checkout.

Ensure calculations work correctly.

## 7. WHATSAPP ORDERING

Create a WhatsApp ordering interface.

When customers check out:
- Display their selected meals.
- Show quantities.
- Calculate the total.
- Generate a WhatsApp order message.

Do not activate ordering using an unconfirmed phone number.

## 8. ABOUT US

Create a short, attractive introduction to Foodie Zone.

Do not invent its founding story, owner, history or achievements.

## 9. CONTACT AND LOCATION

Display the publicly listed Pimville location, clearly indicating that business details require owner verification.

Add:
- Contact number placeholder.
- WhatsApp placeholder.
- Instagram placeholder.
- Google Maps location section.

Do not invent opening hours.

## 10. FINAL QUALITY CHECK

Before completing:
- Test all navigation links.
- Test all menu buttons.
- Test cart functionality.
- Test checkout.
- Test mobile responsiveness.
- Check image quality.
- Check spelling and grammar.
- Remove broken buttons and placeholder links.

## 11. BUSINESS PRESENTATION

The final website must look polished enough to present to the restaurant owner as a professional website proposal from NextWave Web Solutions.

Keep the project as an unofficial demonstration until the owner approves the website, menu, prices, business information and ordering system.
